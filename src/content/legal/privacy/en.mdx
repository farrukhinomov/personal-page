## Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (USA). When you open a page, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data: your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested address, the browser type and the referrer URL. This is required to deliver the pages and to keep the website running and secure.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and error-free operation of the website). The data is stored only as long as this purpose requires.

Because the provider is based in the USA, data may be transferred to a third country. The transfer relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) or on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

## Name resolution (DNS)

I use the DNS service of Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) to resolve the domain name. The Cloudflare proxy is not active for this website: your page requests do not pass through Cloudflare. Cloudflare only processes DNS queries for this domain. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## Storage in your browser

If you change the theme with the switch in the header, your browser stores your choice locally (Local Storage, key `theme`) so it is kept for your next visit. The setting is not sent to me or to third parties. No cookies are set. Access is permitted under § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG because it is necessary for the function you asked for.

## Contact by email

If you email me, I process your sender address and the content of your message to answer your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if the request concerns a contract or preparing one, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. I delete the data once the request is dealt with and no statutory retention duty applies.

Emails to my address are received by the forwarding service ImprovMX (ImprovMX Inc., USA), which forwards them to my mailbox at Google (Gmail). ImprovMX stores incoming emails only briefly on servers in France and deletes them once delivered; forwarding may also run through servers in the USA. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in an email address under my own domain).

## Contact form

You can send me a message with the form on the contact page. I process your name, your email address, optionally your company, the topic you chose, your message and, if you attach any, your files to answer your request. The website only checks attached files for their type and size and forwards them as attachments of the email to me; they are not stored on the website, and the confirmation you receive does not contain them. The legal basis is your consent, which you give with the checkbox in the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). If the request concerns a contract or preparing one, such as a job offer, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR applies as well. You can withdraw your consent at any time by email; this does not affect the lawfulness of the processing before the withdrawal.

The website itself does not store your message. It is delivered to me by email, and you receive a confirmation at the address you entered. It reaches my mailbox at Google (Gmail) through ImprovMX as described above; the message stays there until your request is dealt with and no statutory retention duty applies.

### Sending emails with Resend

To send both emails, I use the Resend service (Resend, Inc., USA) as a processor. Resend processes the content of the emails, including attached files, and the sender and recipient addresses; the data is processed in the EU region Ireland. The legal basis is the same as for the form. Where data is transferred to the USA, the transfer relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) or on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

### Abuse protection with Cloudflare Turnstile

To keep automated programs from misusing the form, I use Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). Turnstile only loads once you use the form, that is, when you click into a field. Cloudflare then processes your IP address and technical characteristics of your browser and device to check that a person is using the form. According to Cloudflare, the data is not used for advertising. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in protection against spam and abuse); access to your device is permitted under § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG because it is necessary to send your message securely. The transfer to the USA relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

### Request limits with Upstash

To prevent abuse, the number of messages per day is limited. For this, I store a counter at Upstash (Upstash, Inc., USA) under a keyed hash (HMAC) of your IP address and, for the confirmation email, of your email address. The addresses themselves are not stored, and the counters are deleted automatically after 24 hours. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in protection against abuse). Where data is transferred to the USA, the transfer relies on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

## Booking a call (intro call)

On the contact page you can request a 30-minute call with me. For this, I process your name, your email address, your company, what you would like to talk about, an optional link (such as to a job posting), the chosen time and your time zone. Scheduling is handled by Cal.com (Cal.com, Inc., USA) as a processor: Cal.com stores the booking, checks it against my Google Calendar and sends you the emails about your request and, once I confirm it, the invitation with the link to Google Meet (Google Ireland Limited). The website loads the free times from Cal.com through my server; your browser does not connect to Cal.com for this.

The legal basis is your consent, which you give with the checkbox in the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), and, where the call concerns a contract or preparing one, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You can withdraw your consent at any time by email. The booking data stays at Cal.com and in my calendar until the call has taken place and no statutory retention duty applies. The transfer to the USA relies on standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

The booking request is also protected by Cloudflare Turnstile and limited to three requests per day and IP address; what is said above about Turnstile and Upstash under contact form applies.

## Links to other websites

This website links to GitHub and LinkedIn. Data is only transferred to the respective provider once you click such a link. The providers' own privacy policies apply to that processing.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). To exercise these rights, write to the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for me is the Unabhängiges Datenschutzzentrum Saarland, Fritz-Dobisch-Str. 12, 66111 Saarbrücken, Germany.

## Changes

I update this policy when the website or the law changes. The version published here applies.
